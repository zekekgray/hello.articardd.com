What is articardd, and why would I use it?

articardd is more than a digital business card. It is your professional digital identity — one place where people can see who you are, what you do, and how they can connect with you.

Instead of giving someone your phone number, email, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, website, and other links separately, you simply give them your articardd. They can instantly view the information you choose to share, save your contact, connect with you, and access your important links.

The biggest advantage is that your information is not static. If your phone number, email, company, position, website, or other information changes, you update your articardd once. The next person who views it sees the latest version — no need to print new cards or send everyone an update.

For professionals, it makes networking faster and more memorable. For entrepreneurs and business owners, it gives you a professional identity you can control and continuously maintain. For salespeople, consultants, freelancers, agents, recruiters, and anyone who regularly meets new people, it makes exchanging information almost effortless.

Instead of asking someone to manually type your details, you can simply say:

“Here, this is my articardd.”

They can open it, see your professional profile, and save your contact.

And unlike relying entirely on social media, articardd gives you a dedicated place for your professional identity. Your social accounts, website, contact information, portfolio, booking links, and other ways of reaching you can all start from one place.

So the value isn't simply that you have a digital version of a business card.

The value is that you have one identity that can stay current, one place people can remember, and one simple way to turn a meeting into a lasting connection.
